queer was a slur

This publication explores the practice of reclaiming queer slurs. Weaving together personal stories, artworks and poetry by eight contributors — all of whom come from different backgrounds and offer different perspectives on slurs — this zine hopes to be a conversation opener and a starting point for deeper research.

HOW TO ZINE

HOW TO ZINE is a space that explores zines as tools for sharing knowledge, building community and learning through making. Started as a series of zine-making sessions with our local community, the project grew into the exhibition setup with a DIY library, a workshop space, a distinct zine-like identity, and a small publication series with practical tips on zine-making.

queer indie publishing manifesto

This graduation research essay took the form of a manifesto and a web-zine. The research focuses on queer independent publishing and its role in community-building. The design plays with the idea of individual VS col­lective authorship by incorporating the quotes of others into main body of text in a collage-like manner.

Hormone therapy digest

The information cards provide a digest of clinical recommendations for people on hormone therapy: what to check, how often, and when to seek extra medical attention. The illustrations break down the scholarly text into digestible bits and guide the reader through the checklist, highlighting the most important parts.

Crossing Border

This playful logo treatment emerged as a proposal for the promo campaign of Crossing Border Festival 2024. As the festival blends international literature with music, the design blends the festival’s mascot bird with the recognisable exit sign — adding a spark of peculiar to the mundane, a little fairytale within our everyday surroundings.

KABK × Rewire Poster Campaign

Experimental poster & flyer designs for Rewire Festival of adventurous music held at The Grey Space in The Hague in 2022. These designs aim to translate the vibrant and dynamic nature of the festival, while playing with legibility and hierarchy of information just like Rewire’s artists play with musical genres’ and instruments’ boundaries — boldly transgressing them.

Was it a Butterfly? Yes, a Moth.

This publication juxtaposes the two narratives of The Silence of the Lambs (the film & the book) to showcase how trans people are represented in each of them and what the difference in perception is. Aside from the fictional story, the margins feature trans people’s voices, offering their reflections on trans representation in these works, and explaining some terms and tropes.

40 years

In 2021 the UK hit the mark of 40 years since the start of the HIV epidemic. This booklet visualises its timeline to acknowledge all the activist work that has been done throughout the years. It serves as a reminder that the epidemic is still ongoing and we should stay aware and well informed about the importance of regular STI tests, timely prevention and treatment.

Stages of work addiction

This journal encapsulates the body of in-depth artistic research about work addiction and its manifestation through visual language — memes in particular. It serves both as an archive of various materials about the topic, and as a representation of the research process behind the project "Stages of work addiction: a guide in memes" (available on request).

MOBILI*ATION

On 24 February 2022, Russia initiated a full-scale invasion in Ukraine. On 21 September 2022, the government started the mobilization of people in Russia, stopping men in the streets and public places to draft them into the army. MOBILI*ATION is the board game that lets you dive into the everyday reality of men in contemporary Russia.
